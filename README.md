# ☀️ SolarFold

> **A foldable, solar-powered portable lamp designed for sustainable lighting anywhere.**

## 📖 Overview

**SolarFold** is a portable lighting system designed to provide reliable illumination while promoting sustainable energy usage. Built with convenience and efficiency in mind, the lamp can be charged using **solar energy** during the day or via a **USB Type-C** connection whenever sunlight isn't available.

Its lightweight, foldable, and compact design makes it an ideal companion for camping, emergency situations, outdoor activities, study sessions during power outages, and everyday portable lighting needs.

---

## ✨ Features

* ☀️ **Solar Charging** – Harness clean, renewable energy to recharge the battery.
* 🔌 **USB Type-C Charging** – Fast and convenient charging whenever required.
* 💡 **Bright LED Illumination** – Energy-efficient lighting with low power consumption.
* 🎒 **Foldable & Portable** – Lightweight design that's easy to carry anywhere.
* 🌱 **Eco-Friendly** – Reduces dependence on disposable batteries and conventional electricity.
* 🔋 **Rechargeable Battery** – Designed for repeated charging cycles and long-term use.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The goal of this project is to develop an affordable, sustainable, and portable lighting solution that encourages the use of renewable energy while providing dependable illumination in both everyday and emergency situations.

---

## 🛠️ Hardware Components

* ☀️ 5V Mini Solar Panel
* 🔋 3.7V 2000mAh Li-Po Battery
* 🔌 TP4056 Charging & Protection Module
* ⚡ MT3608 DC-DC Boost Converter
* 💡 12V LED Strip
* 🎚️ Slide/Toggle Switch
* 🧳 Foldable Compact Housing

## 🌍 Why SolarFold?

Millions of people still experience unreliable electricity or require portable lighting while travelling and exploring outdoors. SolarFold offers a practical alternative by combining renewable solar charging with the convenience of USB Type-C charging, ensuring users always have access to dependable light wherever they go. Its foldable design makes it easy to carry, store, and deploy whenever lighting is needed.
